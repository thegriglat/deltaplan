Deltaplan 1.5.4 — a living world

A few small things that make the world below feel lived in. They follow the flight date: what you see in April is not what you see in August.

World:
- Round hay bales in meadows and fields in summer and autumn, in rows or stacked at the field edge
- Skeins of honking geese in migration months: north in spring, south in autumn, none out of season
- A small seagull charm on the right corner of the control bar, swinging with banking and turbulence

Start from the map:
- You start exactly where you put the point, no more moving to a suitable slope nearby; heading is downhill at that point
