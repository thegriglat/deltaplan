Deltaplan 1.5.4 — Pendulum mode and a living world

Control (experimental):
- New third roll-control mode, "Pendulum" (Settings > Controls): wing and pilot are two bodies, the pilot swings on the hang strap, hands apply force to the bar, sideslip arises on its own. Default is still "Weight shift"; bots and other pilots use the old model.
- Less tested than the older modes. Training and Soviet wings leave a turn on their own in 5-10 s; stalls and spins are modelled.
- Known limits: landing in no wind is hard, tell-tales in a turn deflect inward instead of outward, no VG, the Dutch roll is faster than in real life.

A few small things that make the world below feel lived in. They follow the flight date: what you see in April is not what you see in August.

World:
- Round hay bales in meadows and fields in summer and autumn, in rows or stacked at the field edge
- Skeins of honking geese in migration months: north in spring, south in autumn, none out of season
- A small seagull charm on the right corner of the control bar, swinging with banking and turbulence

Start from the map:
- You start exactly where you put the point, no more moving to a suitable slope nearby; heading is downhill at that point
