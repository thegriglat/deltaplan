# Deltaplan 1.5.4 — Pendulum mode and a living world

11.10.2026 · [Download on itch.io](https://thegriglat.itch.io/deltaplan)

## What's new
**Control: Pendulum mode (experimental)**
- A new third roll-control mode (Settings > Controls): wing and pilot are two bodies, the pilot swings on the hang strap, hands apply force to the bar, and sideslip arises on its own. The default stays the old "Weight shift"; bots and other pilots fly on the old model.
- The mode is less tested than the older ones. Training and Soviet wings leave a turn by themselves in 5-10 s; stalls and spins are modelled (recovery: pull in and shift weight against the rotation).
- Where the simulation lies: landing in no wind is hard; tell-tales in a turn deflect inward instead of outward; there is no VG; the Dutch roll is faster than in real life.

**A living world**
- In summer and autumn round hay bales lie in meadows and fields: in rows (June–September) and in stacks at the field edge (June–November), depending on the flight date.
- In migration months skeins of honking geese fly by: north in spring, south in autumn; no geese out of season.
- A small seagull charm hangs in the right corner of the control bar and swings on its string with banking and turbulence.

**Start from the map**
- You start exactly where you put the point on the map; the game no longer moves it to a suitable slope nearby. Heading is downhill at that point.
