Deltaplan 1.3.4 — a straight launch run in a crosswind

A small fix release. In a crosswind the pilot used to veer off course during the launch run: every little hop at liftoff swung the nose into the wind, and the run went on in the new direction.

Launch:
- While the feet still reach the ground, hops at liftoff no longer turn the pilot (before: 5-10 degrees per hop, up to 40 over the run)
- Once in the harness, the nose lines up with the airflow smoothly, within half a second

Debug:
- F3 wind arrows now use the same colours as F5: red for rising air, blue for sinking air
