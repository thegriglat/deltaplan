# Deltaplan 1.1.0 — a control frame and pilot like a real wing's

05.10.2026 · [Download on itch.io](https://thegriglat.itch.io/deltaplan)

## What's included

**Wing and pilot**

- The control frame is almost perpendicular to the keel: the bottoms of the uprights are forward by 6–13°, different for each wing. The hang point is from the manufacturer's datasheet where it gives one (8 wings), otherwise 1.5 cm ahead of the frame's centre of mass. In trimmed flight the basebar is under the pilot's shoulders.
- The pilot hangs higher: the bottom of the body is 0.37 m above the basebar, arms bent. The wing's tilt in the view matches the flight model.
- At launch the hands are on the uprights at shoulder height, elbows bent; when rolling while standing the wing moves and the feet stay in place; the line runs from the hang point on the keel.
- First person: look down up to 90° (the basebar and hands are visible); the tablet and variometer are on a clamp on the left upright; Q (hold) — look at the instruments, release — the view returns; the ribbons on the front cables are closer to the nose.

**Wings**

- Four classes by crossbar: trainer, fixed crossbar, floating crossbar, topless. In "Flight setup…" each wing shows its class and the class's wind limit: trainer 7–10 m/s, fixed crossbar 10–12, floating crossbar 12–15, topless 20–25.
- Wind in "Flight setup…" can be chosen up to 25 m/s (before, up to 12). The wind neural network is trained up to 8 m/s: above that its field is a rough estimate.

**Interface**

- Loading screen: a frame from the game across the whole screen, the stages over a light dimming; the wind stage is labelled by the actual mode (terrain, neural network, simplified model).
