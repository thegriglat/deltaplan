Deltaplan 1.5.20 — regional trees and houses

Trees and houses now follow the region of the place you fly. Brazil no longer looks like Siberia: you fly over tropical forest and palms, the Mediterranean coast gets oak, pines and terracotta roofs, and Rapa Nui has one-storey tin-roof houses.

World:
- Region chosen from the Koppen-Geiger climate map
- New tree species: oak, palm, tropical broadleaf; tropical canopy stays green in autumn
- Regional houses: tin-roof and stilt houses, round thatched huts, hipped terracotta roofs, flat roofs with parapets
- Altai and the Urals are unchanged

Air model:
- Near-ground wind over land by the sea no longer dies in weak wind with strong heating (cold sea layer is diluted, not carried over)

Goals and training:
- Load XCTrack .xctsk tasks from the Goals tab
- Training modes are cards in the Training window with flight goals

Known issues:
- Species altitude belts are simplified; no trees above about 1700 m in the tropics
- The Southern Hemisphere and Africa use the same three new species
- The Ridge training card counts time aloft, not time on the ridge
