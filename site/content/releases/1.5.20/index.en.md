# Deltaplan 1.5.20 — regional trees and houses

18.10.2026 · [Download on itch.io](https://thegriglat.itch.io/deltaplan)

## What's new
- **Trees and houses follow the region** (Köppen–Geiger climate map): spruce, larch, cedar, pine, oak and birch in the Alps and Caucasus; pine, oak and palms near Nice; tropical forest and palms in the tropics. Altai and the Urals are unchanged. Tropical canopy is evergreen and does not turn yellow in autumn.
- New tree species: oak, palm, tropical broadleaf. Regional houses: tin-roof and stilt houses in the tropics, round thatched huts in the savanna, hipped terracotta roofs by the Mediterranean, flat roofs with parapets in dry regions; Hanga Roa (Rapa Nui) is one storey.
- Near-ground wind over land by the sea no longer dies in weak wind with strong heating because of the cold sea layer (training island "Thermals" launch at 2 m/s: 1.3 m/s at 10 m instead of 0.4). Built-in wind fields re-baked.
- Goals tab: "Load task…" button for `.xctsk` (XCTrack) files.
- Training modes are cards in the Training window with flight goals.
- Honestly: species altitude belts are simplified, no trees above ≈ 1700 m in the tropics; the Southern Hemisphere and Africa use the same three new species; the "Ridge" training card counts time aloft, not time on the ridge.
