Deltaplan 1.5.15 — crash reports

A small update to help hunt down crashes on graphics cards we can't test ourselves.

- If the game closes unexpectedly, on the next start it sends the developer a short report: version, graphics card and driver, last flight settings and the end of the game log
- Paths with your user name are removed from the log
- Nothing to do on your side; the menu shows a short "Crash report sent" line

Known issues:
- Some players see a crash while loading a place with several bots and full wind calculation; workaround: fly with one bot
