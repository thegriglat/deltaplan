# Deltaplan 1.0.2 — wing control and wind over the launch

02.10.2026 · [Download on itch.io](https://thegriglat.itch.io/deltaplan)

## What's included

**Controls**

- The mouse is always the wing (control frame): away from you — nose up, toward you — nose down, sideways — roll. M — mouse capture.
- W/S/A/D no longer move the wing: in flight they turn the head in the cockpit, on the ground — step and turn on the spot.
- Pitch as a hang glider pilot has it: away from you — nose up (braking), toward you — nose down (speeding up).
- Takeoff by physics only: the wing lifts off when it has gained speed, with no time limits. Takeoff is aborted only if a wingtip touches the ground.
- Wing control on the ground: nose and roll — mouse, arrows or stick; with Shift the pilot runs on their own, in an arc toward the roll side.

**Air model**

- Wind from the menu is the wind at 10 m over the launch, the field adjusts to the launch (before, over the launch it came out 1.2–2 times stronger). The "Computing wind" stage became a few seconds longer.
- Turbulence near the ground — by the physics of the surface layer: the wind at the wing at launch no longer swings round in jerks.

**Wings**

- Wings fly by their datasheets (DHV and manufacturer): stall, trim, maximum speed and minimum sink — as in the datasheet.
