Deltaplan 1.5.5 — take-off run in Pendulum mode

Pendulum mode (experimental), take-off run and launch:
- Shorter run on a slope: a training wing lifts off after 10-12 m in no wind and 5-6 m into a headwind.
- Fixed legs that slowed the run on a slope; the hands now hold the wing nose up during the run.
- At launch in a crosswind the wing turns its nose into the wind.
- Known limit: landing in Pendulum mode is still hard even with correct technique.
- The default control mode is still "Weight shift".
