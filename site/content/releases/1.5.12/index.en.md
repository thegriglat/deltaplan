# Deltaplan 1.5.12 — evenings without summer thermals

13.10.2026 · [Download on itch.io](https://thegriglat.itch.io/deltaplan)

## What's new
- Evening and autumn thermals now follow the flight date: the air field was always computed for 15 July, now it uses the date you pick. After sunset the ground no longer heats the air (rocks and built-up areas used to keep "heating" with a delay after sunset), and cloud cover weakens heating once instead of twice. A town cools down over about 1.5 hours after sunset to a weak night-time heat flux. Lift at a slope in strong wind stays: that is flow over the terrain, not a thermal.
- The flight menu wind label now reads "Forecast wind": it is the weather wind, not a reading at launch; at the slope it can be stronger or weaker.
