# Deltaplan 1.3.1 — a straight launch run

07.10.2026 · [Download on itch.io](https://thegriglat.itch.io/deltaplan)

## What's included
**Launch**
- With a "head wind" setting the pilot now faces the actual wind near the ground (the computed field turns the flow along the slope by tens of degrees) instead of the launch heading: the run no longer drifts off course and the crosswind no longer banks the wing. In calm air and without the computed field — as before.
- On the ground a stray mouse roll input returns to neutral: the heading no longer curves after the bank during the run.

**Build**
- itch.io on Linux no longer offers to launch deltaplan.pck instead of the game.
