# Deltaplan 1.5.6 — take-off run and landing

11.10.2026 · [Download on itch.io](https://thegriglat.itch.io/deltaplan)

## What's new
**Take-off run and landing**
- The pilot runs evenly on take-off, without bouncing, in all control modes (the running animation was redone).
- In Pendulum mode, in a crosswind at launch the hands hold the wing on its heading.
- In Pendulum mode, on landing the pilot stands on his feet or runs a few steps; into a headwind the landing is soft.
- Where the simulation lies: in no wind, landing in Pendulum mode is still harder than in real life.
- Flight itself did not change.
