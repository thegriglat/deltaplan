Deltaplan 1.5.6 — take-off run and landing

Take-off run, all control modes:
- The pilot runs evenly, without bouncing (the running animation was redone).

Pendulum mode (experimental):
- In a crosswind at launch the hands hold the wing on its heading.
- On landing the pilot stands on his feet or runs a few steps; into a headwind the landing is soft.

Known issues:
- In no wind, landing in Pendulum mode is still harder than in real life.
- Flight itself did not change.
