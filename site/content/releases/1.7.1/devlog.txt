Deltaplan 1.7.1 — trike fixes

A patch release focused on the trike. The cart no longer lags behind the wing in turns: what you see now matches the physics exactly, so the cart does not twist, pass through the wing or leave the control bar in front of the front strut.

Trike:
- The cart in the air is drawn exactly as the physics computes it, with no lag (cockpit, third-person view and tow tug)
- Cart pitch in the view fixed (was rotated 8.75 degrees nose down)
- The front strut now stops the bar when pushing out; at idle the stop is reached at about 55 km/h
- More pull-in travel: 0.178 m instead of 0.111, pull-in pitch is more responsive
- Grass bends in the propeller wash, up to 25 m behind the trike

Controls:
- Softer mouse bar by default (about 12.7 cm of mouse travel to the stop at 800 DPI)
- Bar sensitivity slider now goes down to x0.1 for high-DPI mice

Known issues:
- Trike pull-in pitch control is still stiff
- No macOS build: the Rust wind library does not build for macOS yet, Mac stays on 1.6.1
