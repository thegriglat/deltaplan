Deltaplan 1.6.1 — crash fix when changing place

A small fix release for pilots. The game no longer crashes when you load another place after a flight.

Fixes:
- Fixed a crash when loading another place after a flight (for example Kok-Tobe, then the menu, then Yutsa): cars, trees, landing sites, the dog, geese and bots of the previous place still used its unloaded terrain
- Fixed "Out of bounds get index" errors after changing place: background placement of rocks and forest no longer reads the surface mask while it is being changed
