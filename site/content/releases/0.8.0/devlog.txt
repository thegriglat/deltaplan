Deltaplan 0.8.0 — flying together

The big one: you can now fly with friends in the same sky. Everyone gets the same world — the same thermals, the same clouds, the same day — so "I'm under that big cloud over the ridge" actually means something.

Network play:
===
- Network game in the main menu: Create picks a site and gives you a 4-digit code, friends Join with that code
- Same room: leave the server field empty and the zone runs on your computer; friends on the same home network see it under Nearby and join with one key
- Over the internet: run the small server yourself (Go, one Docker command) and enter its address
- The first pilot leads the zone; if they leave, the next one takes over without anyone noticing
- Catch up: press = to see the pilots in the zone, Enter to glide smoothly over the terrain to the chosen pilot in about ten seconds and take over control next to them
- After landing or a crash: Continue nearby, or walk back to launch
- Launch queue like on a real hill: pilots first, then bots; stall at the front for a minute and you go to the back, a failed launch sends you to the back too
- Bots fly in network zones as well, the same bots for everyone
- Your pilot name, shown above your wing
===

Flying and weather:
===
- Every new flight is a new day: same forecast, different thermals and clouds; Fly again replays the same day
- Thunderstorms are rare now: almost never at +26 C, occasional on hot days
- Half of the other pilots are already in the air when you arrive at launch, the rest launch after you
===

Other:
===
- New icon and startup screen
- Smoother sky and haze gradients
- Shaders are prepared in advance: faster first start and fewer hitches early in the flight
===

Known issues:
===
- Network play was tested on Linux; please report anything odd on Windows
- In network play a far cloud at the edge of view can occasionally vanish at once
===
