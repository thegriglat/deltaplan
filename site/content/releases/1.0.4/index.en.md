# Deltaplan 1.0.4 — simplified wind as in 0.8.0

03.10.2026 · [Download on itch.io](https://thegriglat.itch.io/deltaplan)

## What's included

**Wind**

- In the "Wind over terrain → Simplified" mode the wind grows with height as in version 0.8.0: a power-law profile, no more than ×1.8 of the wind at 10 m, with no dependence on time of day and cloud cover. For example, with 3 m/s wind at the ground, at 300 m above the terrain it is about 4.8 m/s (in 1.0.3 at clear noon it was about 4.2). The slope, lee zones, thermals and turbulence in simplified mode are the same as in 0.8.0.
- The "Calculated from terrain" mode is unchanged.
