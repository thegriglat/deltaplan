Deltaplan 1.5.7 — motion rig fix

A fix for motion platforms after the first real test with a DOF Reality rig through Sim Racing Studio.

Motion rig (SRS):
- Heave now moves the platform: accelerations are sent in m/s² instead of g (before, they were about 1% of the scale).
- Motion data is sent on the ground too: slope, take-off run, touchdown and roll-out.
- No more jump at take-off and landing from the stream switching on and off.
- Gain and filters are set in Sim Racing Studio.

Known issues:
- Touchdown is stiff in the physics: the landing impact is short and strong.
- Heave and ground motion are not yet tested on a real platform.
