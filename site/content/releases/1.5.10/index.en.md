# Deltaplan 1.5.10 — a living launch, instruments on the wrists, new menu backgrounds

13.10.2026 · [Download on itch.io](https://thegriglat.itch.io/deltaplan)

## What's new
**Pilot: what you see and hear**
- Quiet background sound in the menu: mountain wind with slow gusts and lulls, and now and then a distant cry of an eagle, a kite or a buzzard. It fades out when the flight starts and comes back in the menu.
- Bar pressure is shown by the thickness of the harness indicator frame: almost zero at trim speed, heavier when speeding up, almost none in a steady turn.
- A wristwatch on the left hand and a compass on the right, visible in first person when the hands are in view: the hands show the game time of day, the compass card shows the heading with the lag of a liquid compass. In third person the time is shown as text at the bottom right. Both can be turned off in the settings.
- Gloves without gauntlets, just the hands; the hand on the base bar stays fixed relative to the bar.
- Your own legs in first person: the pilot leaves the pod earlier (below 25 m), the legs are at the bottom of the view.
- On landing the ground under and slightly ahead of the wing darkens: the wing hides part of the sky. The lower you are, the smaller, denser and sharper the patch; it is stronger under overcast skies.
- Feet rustle in the grass when walking or dragging on landing; quiet on rock, water, snow and in towns.

**At the launch**
- Tents are closer to the launch, 30-50 m behind or to the side, never on the run-up or down the slope; no tents on steep slopes or at cliffs.
- Grass is trampled around the tents, the gear, the run-up and the path to the launch.
- Every launch has the gear of a flying trip: two to four rigged gliders standing nose into the wind, a half-assembled glider, bags, harnesses, helmets, bottles. You can crash into them.
- Tents, bags, sail and clothes are real ripstop fabric with folds and dust.
- People at the camp are no longer capsules: up close they walk, stand and wave; far away they are simple figures.
- Other pilots wait for their turn away from the tents and gear; in network play the camp is the same for everyone.

**Menu**
- 16 new menu backgrounds taken in the game, changing every 20 s with a smooth fade: forested mountains of Altai and the Urals, Everest, Elbrus, Matterhorn, Mont Blanc, Fuji, the glider's shadow on a meadow. The same frames are used on the loading screen. No text on the pictures.

**Graphics**
- The telltales sit exactly on the front wire of any glider: a smooth satin ribbon looped around the wire, with a frayed end.
- Control frame wheels: a treaded tyre, a spoked plastic rim and a hub.

**Fixes**
- Other pilots no longer appear right behind the player at the launch (between the camera and the player).
- White glowing "lamps" on the frame fittings (nose, control frame corners) are gone; the metal still shines.
