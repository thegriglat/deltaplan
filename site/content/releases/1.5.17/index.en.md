# Deltaplan 1.5.17 — no grass on rock

16.10.2026 · [Download on itch.io](https://thegriglat.itch.io/deltaplan)

## What's new
- Grass no longer grows on the bare terrain rock: no grass on cliffs, rock outcrops or scree.
- Mid-range grass cards are now low strips with ragged tops made of separate blades; they draw less often, without a density jump near the blades, and without dark patches at the wheel and the tethers.
- Grass costs no more than in 1.5.16.
