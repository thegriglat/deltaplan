# Deltaplan 1.7.4 — the pilot voice sounds human now

06.11.2026 · [Download on itch.io](https://thegriglat.itch.io/deltaplan)

## What's new
- **Pilot voice re-recorded.** Russian no longer sounds foreign or like an audiobook: each voice now has its own reference in each language, made of short casual remarks. The male voice is now in his forties.
- **New lines.** Casual, written separately for English and Russian instead of translated, with some humour and a bit of swearing — "Holy shit!" in a stall, "Crap, crap, crap." in sink, "Back off, buddy!" to an eagle. Swearing is said with annoyance instead of a flat voice.
- **Place selection.** "Launch set on terrain…" and "Reset" are on their own row: the label used to shrink and letters stacked vertically.

## Known issues
- Annoyed lines sound noticeably different in timbre from the other lines of the same voice; not all lines have been checked by ear.
- No macOS build: the Rust wind library does not build for macOS yet, Mac stays on 1.6.1.
