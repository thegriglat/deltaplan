Deltaplan 1.7.4 — the pilot voice sounds human now

A small patch for the pilot voice. Russian lines no longer sound like a foreigner reading an audiobook, and the pilot talks the way a real pilot does: short casual remarks, a joke now and then, and a swear word when things go wrong.

Pilot voice:
- Re-recorded both voices: each voice has its own reference in each language, made of short casual remarks
- New lines, written separately for English and Russian instead of translated
- Some humour and a bit of swearing: "Holy shit!" in a stall, "Crap, crap, crap." in sink, "Back off, buddy!" to an eagle
- Swearing is said with annoyance instead of a flat voice

Fixes:
- Place selection: "Launch set on terrain..." and "Reset" are on their own row, the label no longer shrinks into a column of letters

Known issues:
- Annoyed lines sound noticeably different in timbre from the other lines of the same voice
- No macOS build: the Rust wind library does not build for macOS yet, Mac stays on 1.6.1
