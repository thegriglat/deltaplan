# Deltaplan 1.1.1 — a clear launch picker map

05.10.2026 · [Download on itch.io](https://thegriglat.itch.io/deltaplan)

## What's included
**Launch point picker**
- Instead of the hillshade — an ordinary OpenStreetMap map with village and town names, roads and rivers. A switch turns on the OpenTopoMap topographic map with contour lines. Tiles are cached on disk, the map source is credited in the corner.
- For a point selected on the map, the elevation above sea level in metres is shown next to the coordinates — both on the map and in the point's caption on the "Flight setup…" screen.
