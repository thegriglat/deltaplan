# Deltaplan 1.0.3 — choice of wind model

03.10.2026 · [Download on itch.io](https://thegriglat.itch.io/deltaplan)

## What's included

**Settings**

- New item "Wind over terrain": "Calculated from terrain" (as before) or "Simplified". In simplified mode the wind field is not computed on the graphics card, the "Computing wind" stage is skipped, and wind and thermals follow the simplified model. If the game crashes on loading or in flight, you can switch to "Simplified" and keep flying on the latest version without rolling back to an old one.
- The choice is saved between launches and takes effect from the next place load. In multiplayer, everyone has their own.

**Wings**

- The Apogee — area 14.4 m² instead of 16 (a copy of the Airwave Magic 155; 16 m² were only a few individual units), the wing model has a smaller chord. It flies slightly faster: trim ≈ 34 km/h instead of 32, stall ≈ 26 instead of 24.5, minimum sink 1.37 m/s; the same glide ratio (≈ 7).
