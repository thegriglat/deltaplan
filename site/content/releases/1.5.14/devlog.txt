Deltaplan 1.5.14 — new look of mountains and forest

The scenery got a big overhaul: forest, meadows, snow, light and the mountains themselves look closer to what you see from a glider. Seasons now follow the flight date, latitude and altitude. Also includes 1.5.13: the game takes almost half the disk space.

Forest and meadows:
- Distant forest has volume and no visible switch ring; near forest rebuilds several times faster
- Large tone patches on meadows, lusher grass near water, tree line by latitude, alpine meadow above it
- Cattle trails on steep meadows, wind gusts show as streaks on the grass
- Sail and foliage glow against the sun

Seasons and snow:
- Spring grass, summer burn-out, golden autumn, bare crowns; fields from ploughland to stubble
- Fresh snow line and glaciers by date and latitude; snow does not stick to steep rock

Mountains and light:
- Sharper ridges and rocks as in Copernicus data; distant peaks no longer sink; no cracks at seams
- Sky and ground light the slopes differently; mountain shadows keep the blue sky light
- Mountain shadow now also falls around the pilot
- Haze depends on direction to the sun

Performance and size:
- Low preset is noticeably lighter (own terrain shader variant); no preset is more expensive than before
- Game data 212 MB -> 86 MB (compressed glider textures)

Known issues:
- A faint light ring around the pilot can still show on snow in mountain shadow
