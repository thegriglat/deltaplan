# Deltaplan 1.5.14 — new look of mountains and forest

14.10.2026 · [Download on itch.io](https://thegriglat.itch.io/deltaplan)

## What's new
- Distant forest has volume and no visible switch ring: far trees are drawn as volumetric crosses up to 900/1100/1500 m depending on the preset; near forest rebuilds several times faster.
- Meadows with large tone patches, lusher grass near water, tree line depends on latitude, alpine meadow above it.
- Seasons by date, latitude and altitude: spring grass, summer burn-out, golden autumn, bare crowns; fields go through phases from ploughland to stubble.
- Fresh snow and glaciers by date and latitude; snow does not stick to steep rock, ragged edge.
- High mountains are sharper: ridges and rocks as in Copernicus data, distant peaks no longer sink, no cracks at mesh seams.
- More natural light: sky and ground light the slopes differently, mountain shadows keep the blue sky light, haze depends on direction to the sun; mountain shadow now also falls around the pilot.
- Sail and foliage glow against the sun; cattle trails on steep meadows; rocks and screes without repeating patterns; wind gusts show as streaks on the grass.
- Low preset is noticeably lighter: its own terrain shader variant without the expensive details; no preset is more expensive than before.
- Menu and loading backgrounds re-shot.
