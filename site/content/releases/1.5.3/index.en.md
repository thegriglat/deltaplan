# Deltaplan 1.5.3 — cars on the roads

11.10.2026 · [Download on itch.io](https://thegriglat.itch.io/deltaplan)

## What's new
**Cars on the roads**
- Cars drive along OpenStreetMap roads: cars, vans and trucks in different colours. Most of them are on motorways and main roads; none appear in yards or on dirt tracks.
- Each car has its own speed; when it catches up with a slower one it follows at a distance, it slows down before turns, and trucks are slower. At a dead end a car turns around.
- Cars only exist within ~1.5 km of the pilot; settings and the on/off switch are in `world_objects.json → osm_traffic`.
