Deltaplan 1.5.3 — cars on the roads

The roads below you are no longer empty: cars, vans and trucks now drive along OpenStreetMap roads around the pilot. It is simple traffic, just enough to make a road look alive when you fly over it.

Traffic:
- Cars, vans and trucks in different colours
- Most traffic on motorways and main roads, less on local streets, none in yards or on dirt tracks
- Each car has its own speed, follows a slower car at a distance, slows down before turns; trucks are slower
- At the end of a road a car goes the straightest way on; at a dead end it turns around
- Cars only within about 1.5 km of the pilot; settings in world_objects.json, section osm_traffic

Known issues:
- No traffic rules at junctions: cars pass through each other
- A car changes lanes abruptly when it turns around at a dead end
