Deltaplan 1.0.0 — air shaped by the terrain, 48 gliders

The air is now computed from the terrain for every flight. While the flight loads, the game solves the wind field over the hills for your start hour, wind and weather, and then thermals, turbulence and the air behind ridges all come from that field. Ridge lift, venturi in saddles, rotor in the lee and morning inversions behave the way pilots know them, checked against field measurements (Askervein, Perdigao).

And there are 48 gliders now: 39 new ones built from DHV and manufacturer data — trainers, kingposted and topless wings.

Air model:
- Wind field over the terrain for every flight, refreshed every 15 game minutes and when the wind changes
- Thermals from the field: more over sunny slopes and convergence lines, none in shade, capped by the morning inversion
- Turbulence from the field: stronger with wind and at the edge, almost gone in stable morning air
- Lee side and rotor follow the field and field measurements instead of a fixed rule; no more -18 m/s sink behind ridges
- Wind gradient depends on time of day and cloud cover
- Grass and water ripples react more at the edge than in the valley

Gliders:
- 39 new gliders, 48 in total: Wills Wing, Moyes, Aeros, Icaro, Airborne, Seedwings and more
- Span, area, nose angle, battens, kingpost, glider weight and pilot range from DHV and manufacturer data; the polar comes from a glider of the same class scaled to wing loading
- Falcon, Litespeed, Combat and Laminar corrected to their data sheets
- On the ground the pilot holds the wing level; a strong side gust can lift a tip and flip it
- Every glider with its data and sources on the website. Thanks to DHV for the open type test data

World:
- Launching from a map point in the forest works: trees are cleared around the launch
- Grass under the trees, smoother tree detail changes, 8 different trees of each kind
- Cirrus clouds as long bands of thin streaks
- A few small surprises in the world for those who look around

Menu and graphics:
- Main menu opens instantly, the world loads after Fly
- Start time: 9:00, 12:00, 15:00 or 20:00
- Softer, more natural colors; 4K menu background
- Debug overlays for flight reviews: F1/F2 performance, F3/F5 wind arrows, F6 thermals
- About 17 MB smaller download thanks to better terrain compression

Known issues:
- The wind field needs a GPU with compute support; without it the game uses the older simpler wind
- Turbulence above the wake behind a ridge in strong wind is still too strong (about 3x)
- Small hills can show a lee bubble where real air would not separate
