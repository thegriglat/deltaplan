# Deltaplan 1.5.1 — a world from the satellite map

10.10.2026 · [Download on itch.io](https://thegriglat.itch.io/deltaplan)

## What's new
**Place data without OpenStreetMap**
- Any point in the world is built without OpenStreetMap: only terrain and the satellite land cover map are needed. No more long waits and server failures while a place loads.
- Villages are houses with sheds and saunas inside built-up patches from the satellite map; village edges are soft.
- Lakes and rivers come from the satellite map and terrain.
- There are no more roads, power lines or fences; the path from the launch goes down the slope.
- Built-in places (Altai, Ongudai, Askarovo, Aushkul) are downloaded on first use and then open from the cache.
- The loading screen shows a request counter, so you can see how much is left.

**Pilot and camera**
- Pilot pose in flight: upper arms down, elbows out "like on a motorbike", forearms to the bar; the pilot hangs with the centre of mass under the carabiner. The model's upper arm now has normal human proportions.
- Two cockpit views: the pilot's eyes and behind without the pilot (default).

**Other**
- Bots waiting for launch each stand in their own spot and walk to the launch one at a time.
- The game is almost half the size: the archive went from 168 to about 90 MB.
