Deltaplan 1.5.1 — a world from the satellite map

The game no longer depends on OpenStreetMap. Every place, anywhere in the world, is now built only from terrain and the satellite land cover map: villages are generated inside built-up patches, lakes come straight from the satellite map. Loading a new place no longer waits for an overloaded map server, and the loading screen shows how many requests are left.

The pilot also got a new pose in flight, closer to real flying: upper arms down, elbows out, forearms to the bar, hanging with the centre of mass under the carabiner. And the game is almost half the size.

Places:
- No OpenStreetMap: terrain and satellite land cover only
- Villages with houses, sheds and saunas inside built-up areas
- Lakes and rivers from the satellite map and terrain
- No roads, power lines or fences; a path goes down from the launch
- Built-in places are downloaded on first use, then open from the cache
- Request counter on the loading screen

Pilot and camera:
- New flying pose: elbows out like on a motorbike, forearms to the bar
- Pilot hangs with the centre of mass under the carabiner; normal arm proportions
- Two cockpit views: pilot's eyes, and behind without the pilot (default)

Other:
- Bots waiting for launch keep their own spots and go one at a time
- Download is almost half the size: about 90 MB instead of 168 MB

Known issues:
- The first visit to a built-in place needs the internet (about a minute)
- Takeoff in a strong headwind can drop a wingtip on some gliders
