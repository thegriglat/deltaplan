# Deltaplan 1.5.16 — new grass and speed feel

15.10.2026 · [Download on itch.io](https://thegriglat.itch.io/deltaplan)

## What's new
- Grass: close-up blades are volumetric again. At mid range, looking along a gentle slope, the meadow draws as blade cards, and when the slope is steep, as layers; the two blend smoothly. The far ring is gone, and grass costs about 0.2–0.3 ms less per frame where it is visible.
- Rocks are now convex polyhedra with fewer faces, which is cheaper, and their surface detail is triplanar.
- The Pendulum roll mode is the default and the first one in settings. Unknown values in the config fall back to it.
- Speed feel: turbulence shakes the cockpit more often as airspeed grows, the tether ribbon flutters with speed, the tether whistle is now synthesized from airspeed, and the head follows the wing pitch by 0.3 with a short lag.
