Deltaplan 1.5.16 — new grass and speed feel

Grass is back to volumetric blades up close. On mid range it now draws as blade cards when you look along a gentle slope and as layers on steep ground, blending smoothly between them. The far ring is gone, so grass costs less per frame.

Rocks are convex polyhedra with fewer faces, and the Pendulum roll mode is now the default.

- Grass: close blades, cards on gentle slopes, layers on steep ground; far ring removed, about 0.2-0.3 ms cheaper where grass is visible
- Rocks: convex low-poly shapes, triplanar surface detail
- Pendulum is the default roll mode and the first in settings
- Turbulence shakes the cockpit more often as airspeed grows
- Tether ribbon flutters more with speed
- Tether whistle is synthesized from airspeed
- The head follows the wing pitch by 0.3 with a short lag
