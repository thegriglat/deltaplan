# Deltaplan 0.7.0 — a new pilot and cloud shadows

28.09.2026. {{< button href="https://thegriglat.itch.io/deltaplan" >}}Download on itch.io{{< /button >}}

## What's included

- New pilot: a real person (jacket, trousers, hang point) instead of a figure made of primitives; leather boots with
  a contoured sole (previously a bare foot on a black block); a helmet with a curved tinted visor that reflects
  the sky; gloves with gauntlets, fingers wrapped around the basebar and uprights.
- Names above other pilots: visible in the air and from the cockpit, hidden behind a mountain. Settings → "Pilot names"
  (on by default).
- Cloud shadows as in real life: they follow the cloud shape (puffs, thinning edges) and dim the direct sun rather than
  tinting the ground — on a sunlit slope the shadow is noticeable, in the shade of a mountain it is not "doubled"; they
  fall on grass, forest, bushes and rocks, and move with the clouds. Now also on "Low".
- The red ribbons were moved to the front cables of the control frame (they were on the side ones).
- A control frame like the real one: the uprights joint under the keel with bolts, a hang point with a D-carabiner and a red
  safety loop, control frame corners on plates with bolts, round cables with thimbles; rubber grips on the bar (on
  Soviet wings — a bare tube); topless wings get teardrop-shaped uprights, curved at the bottom, and a speedbar.
- A campfire in the pilots' camp at the launch: stones, logs, fire; thin smoke is carried by the local wind — in calm air
  a thread going straight up, in wind it leans and trails along the ground.
- Water feels the wind: in calm air the lake is a mirror, with wind it is matte from ripples; gusts run across the water
  as dark patches ("cat's paws"), and by the upwind shore there is a smooth light strip; the sun glint on ripples is softer.
