# Deltaplan 1.5.21 — pull-in speed by the data sheets

20.10.2026 · [Download on itch.io](https://thegriglat.itch.io/deltaplan)

## What's new
- **Full pull-in speed matches the glider data sheets.** Wing pitch stiffness is now nonlinear: softer in the working range, stronger at low angles of attack (reflex lines). Median over 48 wings is 0.98 of the data-sheet top speed without VG (was 0.69–0.73); trim speed is unchanged.
- **Pull to the hips:** hold Shift (right bumper on a gamepad) to pull the bar past the normal full pull-in, down to the hips (0.7 m). Past the normal limit the harness indicator turns red; release Shift and the limit returns smoothly. A homemade control bar gets the full travel directly.
- **The wing is flown with the mouse, gamepad stick or homemade control bar only.** Keyboard pitch and roll are gone; on the ground the keyboard walks, runs and turns. Service keys are unchanged.
- **Letting the bar out without tumbling:** when you let the bar out after pulling in, the pilot's arms relax and the wing returns to trim by itself. Instant release from 1.8× trim speed: 0 tumbles out of 48 wings (was 29). In normal flight the arms still hold, roll handling is unchanged.
- Less video memory for oak and tropical trees.
- Honestly: no VG. Letting out from top speed with the hip pull-in (above about 2× trim) often ends in a zoom and a tumble — that is physics, let the bar out more gently. The sport wing does not recover from a spin; the cause is not found yet.
