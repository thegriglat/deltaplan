Deltaplan 1.5.21 — pull-in speed by the data sheets

Full pull-in now gives the top speed from the glider data sheets. Before, the sport wings topped out 25-30% short; now the median over 48 wings is 0.98 of the data-sheet speed without VG. For the last bit of speed, hold Shift and pull the bar to your hips.

Flight:
- Nonlinear wing pitch stiffness: softer in the working range, stronger at low angles of attack (reflex lines); trim speed unchanged
- Letting the bar out after pull-in no longer tumbles the wing: the pilot's arms relax and the wing returns to trim (0 of 48 wings tumble on instant release from 1.8x trim, was 29)

Controls:
- Hold Shift (right bumper on a gamepad) to pull the bar to the hips (0.7 m); the harness indicator turns red past the normal limit
- Gamepad stick: full travel to the chest, to the hips with the bumper; homemade control bar: full travel to the hips
- No keyboard pitch or roll any more: the wing is flown with the mouse, gamepad or control bar; on the ground the keyboard walks and runs

Graphics:
- Less video memory for oak and tropical trees

Known issues:
- No VG
- Letting out from top speed with the hip pull-in (above about 2x trim) often zooms into a tumble - that is physics, let the bar out gently
- The sport wing does not recover from a spin
