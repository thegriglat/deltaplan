# Deltaplan 1.5.11 — legs lower, trees in town

13.10.2026 · [Download on itch.io](https://thegriglat.itch.io/deltaplan)

## What's new
- The pilot leaves the pod below 8 m when descending (was 25 m) and goes back above 15 m: no more jumping out of the harness all the time while ridge soaring along a slope.
- In towns, tree models now stand in yards and parks where the ground shows tree crowns (away from buildings and roads), and the painted crowns next to them fade out, so trees are no longer doubled. Forests outside towns are unchanged.
- Menu and loading backgrounds were retaken: they show the whole pilot, not just hands and legs.
