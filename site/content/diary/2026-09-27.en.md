On the first day an MVP was assembled (≈250 commits): flight by polar curve, terrain of real places, thermals, clouds, ridge lift, rotors, instruments, sounds and menus — four builds in one day. Pilot feedback went straight into fixes: roll, sail, trees, visibility.

<!--more-->

## Project start and MVP

(≈250 commits): requirements and plan, a flight model based on the polar curve, terrain from real elevations (Ongudai, Askarovo, Aushkul, Manzherok), thermals with a life cycle, clouds, ridge lift, rotors, instruments (tablet, a 90s variometer), sounds, menus. Builds: MVP, evening, night, 0.5.0 (other pilots in the sky, tents, the sun dazzles). The consulting pilot flew on Windows all day.

## Pilot feedback

Pilot feedback of this day: roll control "very real, I even landed on my feet" (weight-shift mode by default); the sail doesn't flutter ("it only fluttered in the 70s"); trees up close "like palms" → redone; "softer in the distance" → visibility 35 km.
