## Builds 0.6.0 → 0.7.0 → 0.7.1

- 0.6.0: weather from a forecast (temperature, wind, cloud cover instead of "weak/medium/strong day"), the day follows the sun, rotor behind the ridge, English language, 9 real wings in 4 classes, a ribbon on the cables, gloves.
- 0.7.0: ripples on water from wind ("cat's paws"), cloud shadows following the cloud shape (they cut off direct sun), a campfire by the tents with smoke drifting with the wind, a realistic control frame, a new pilot on MPFB2 (CC0), names above the bots; then boots and a visor (the consulting pilot: "barefoot, and on top a black block").
- 0.7.1: grainy ripple on models in the wing's shadow — the sun penumbra 0.8° → 0.25° (physically the sun is ~0.53°; above ~0.3° PCSS without TAA gives noise).
- A folder `site/content/releases/<version>/` with screenshots and a devlog — at the user's request.

## Multiplayer — from estimate to roadmap in one evening

No lobby needed; "Multiplayer": place → 4-digit code; join by code; a host with handover; "Catch up" (the = key, a tow flight by a speed "dome" of ~10 s); a queue for launch (a failed launch → to the end of the queue — intentionally); bots at the host. Transport: a Telegram bot and mesh/DHT were considered and rejected; a Go server on a VPS + a built-in server for a single room ("they will sit in one room, it's easier for them to meet in person"); the contract is .proto over WebSocket (there is no gRPC in Godot). The world string `deltaplan://world?...` by coordinates (the place name is personal, coordinates are deterministic).

## World determinism (NET-00)

The atmosphere became a pure function of place, date, weather and time; every "Fly" is a new random seed ("Again" — the same day).
