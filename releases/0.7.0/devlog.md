# Deltaplan 0.7.0 — reading the air

This update is about the small things pilots look at without thinking: shadows on the slope, ripples on a lake, smoke at camp, and the glider right in front of your face.

## Cloud shadows you can fly by

Cloud shadows are now cast by the clouds themselves. Each shadow has the real shape of its cloud, including the billows and the thinning edges of a dying cumulus, and it drifts across the ground with the cloud.

A shadow now blocks direct sunlight instead of just darkening the ground. On a sunlit slope it reads clearly from altitude, and a slope already in the shade of a mountain doesn't turn doubly dark. Thermals are weaker under the same shadows, so what you see is what the air does. Shadows now work on the Low graphics preset too.

## Water that shows the wind

Lakes and rivers now respond to the wind:

- **Calm:** the lake is a mirror.
- **As the wind picks up:** ripples turn the water matte.
- **Gusts:** they run across the surface as dark patches, the "cat's paws" sailors watch for.
- **The upwind shore:** it keeps a smooth, bright strip in the wind shadow.

The sun's glint on the water is softer now that it breaks up on the ripples.

## Campfire at launch

The pilots' camp near launch now has a campfire: a ring of stones, a few logs, and a small fire. The smoke is thin and follows the local wind. It rises straight up in calm air, leans over in a breeze, and lies down low when it's blowing hard.

## A real control frame

The control frame has been rebuilt on all nine gliders. It is what you look at most of the flight, so it now has the detail of a real one:

- a bolted apex bracket with the main hang strap, a D-carabiner and a red backup loop;
- bolted corner plates;
- round flying wires with thimbles and swaged fittings;
- grips on the base bar (bare tube on the Soviet-era wings).

Topless wings get faired uprights and a speed bar.

The red telltales now hang on the front wires.

## A new pilot

The old mannequin is gone. The pilot is now a proper human figure in a jacket and trousers, with a harness and an open-face helmet with a visor. The gloves have real fingers that wrap around the base bar in flight and the uprights on the ground.

## Names over other pilots

Every other pilot in the air now has a name label: Russian names in the Russian version, English names in the English one. The labels show from the cockpit too and are hidden behind terrain. You can turn them off in **Settings → Pilot names**.

## Known issues

- On a few glider and site combinations in strong wind, the autopilot launch can still fail on a tailwind gust.
- The pilot's clothing has no folds yet, and the harness looks stiff when standing.

Thanks for flying, and for the feedback from pilots that keeps shaping this.
